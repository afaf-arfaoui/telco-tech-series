MicroVMs, eBPF, and AI Agents in Telecom Infrastructure

Dr. Afaf Arfaoui · September 09, 2026 · …

This series is about watching several technologies (microVMs, eBPF, and AI agents) actually work together in telecom infrastructure, through real working examples rather than abstract descriptions. Along the way, episodes explain just enough of the underlying tech to make sense of what is happening, then build on it.

It begins with the microVM, what one actually is, and how to deploy it by hand. From there it moves through handing that deployment to an AI agent, and eventually running and monitoring a real telecom workload, an eBPF-based UPF, inside one of these microVMs.

No need to go in order. Feel free to skip whichever episodes cover something you already know. Each one is written to stand reasonably on its own.

About the author

Dr. Afaf Arfaoui

Afaf Arfaoui is standardization researcher at DOCOMO, a Japanese mobile telecommunications company, which is a subsidiary of NTT, a prominent player in the global telecommunications industry. Prior to her role as a standardization researcher, Afaf earned her Ph.D. in telecommunications, where she concentrated on enhancing the allocation of network resources in both the RAN and Cloud networks. She also holds an engineering degree in ICT. When she isn't immersed in standardizing technologies or conducting research, she is hiking in the Alps or baking cakes in her kitchen.
